Meet the founder

Mohammad Amaan — builder behind ZendrosCloud

My interest in cloud computing actually started with gaming. I was running games I couldn't run locally through cloud gaming platforms like Gloud Games, and got curious about what was actually happening on the other end of that connection. That curiosity turned into a computer science degree in Bhilai and a cloud computing certification from IIT Kharagpur, which gave me the theory to go with it.

Hands-on training at Bhilai Steel Plant showed me what unplanned downtime really costs at scale, and a DevOps internship at Elevate Labs taught me how to build systems that catch failures before they become outages. I started ZendrosCloud because most startups can't hire a full platform team — and they shouldn't have to just to get infrastructure that holds up.
